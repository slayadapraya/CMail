Work in progress.

- **Sync fix:** Calendar and Contacts now load independently of inbox imports, errors, and Gmail quota pauses. Queued Calendar refreshes are preserved; Calendar has its own refresh button.
- **compact layout:** cleaner header, icon toolbar, sidebar, and app surfaces, with a CMail Blue preset.
- **Inbox size:** choose current-sized cards or compact rows. Movable tabs and standalone windows remain supported.

**Install/update:** download the `.deb`, close CMail, then run:
```bash
sudo apt install ./cmail_0.8.1_all.deb
```
